# ImpactIQ Demo Video Script

**Target Length:** 3:00  
**Pacing:** Maintain a conversational, steady pace. Do not speed-read.

---

### 0:00–0:12 | Hook
*(Visual: Orbit view already animating. No logo slide yet.)*

**Voiceover:**  
"Ever seen a headline that says an asteroid 'might hit Earth' and had no idea whether to actually be worried?"

### 0:12–0:25 | Contrast
*(Visual: Briefly show a generic AI chat screen giving a vague answer.)*

**Voiceover:**  
"Ask a chatbot and it'll either panic you or shrug it off — it's not running the numbers."

### 0:25–0:35 | Premise + name
*(Visual: Product name on screen, transitioning to the main search interface.)*

**Voiceover:**  
"So we built something that runs the real physics first, and only lets AI explain what it found. This is ImpactIQ."

### 0:35–1:35 | The one object — animated scrubber doing the work
*(Visual: Search for Bennu live. Use the animated orbit + time-scrubber. Scrub forward and watch the Monte Carlo cloud resolve in real time as you approach the close-approach date. Risk score and Torino badge land as the scrubber reaches the date.)*

**Voiceover:**  
"Let’s look at Bennu, currently on NASA's watch list. 

Instead of pulling a static number, our backend runs a live Monte Carlo simulation. As we scrub forward in time, watch the uncertainty cloud resolve. Thousands of trajectories are calculated, landing on an empirical impact probability and a concrete risk score right at the exact date of close approach."

### 1:35–1:55 | AI brief, with Guardian folded in
*(Visual: Scroll down to the AI Mission Brief. The text appears, and the 'Guardian pass' seal lights up next to it.)*

**Voiceover:**  
"We feed those structured numbers into an IBM Granite model to generate a clear, calibrated mission brief—and a second Guardian model checks that before you ever see it. And it explains itself just as honestly to a ten-year-old as it does to a scientist."

### 1:55–2:10 | One follow-up question
*(Visual: Type a natural follow-up into the chat interface: "when will we actually know for sure?" The grounded answer lands.)*

**Voiceover:**  
"If you want to know more, you can just ask it. *'When will we actually know for sure?'* Because it's chatting directly with the calculated data, you get a grounded answer based entirely on the physics."

### 2:10–2:40 | The trust moment
*(Visual: Split screen or hover to show the live comparison: our Monte Carlo probability next to JPL Sentry's published probability for Bennu.)*

**Voiceover:**  
"Here's the part that actually matters — JPL already publishes their own official number for this object. 

Watch: our live calculation lands in the exact same order of magnitude as theirs. The physics engine isn't making this up."

### 2:40–2:52 | One breadth flash
*(Visual: A fast flash through the multi-object dashboard ranking a few real Sentry objects by Insight Score.)*

**Voiceover:**  
"And it scales instantly to triage the rest of the solar system."

### 2:52–3:00 | Mission close
*(Visual: Clean closing screen with the ImpactIQ logo, team members' names, and a GitHub link.)*

**Voiceover:**  
"We're not trying to tell you asteroids are scary. We're trying to tell you whether this one actually is. Thank you."
